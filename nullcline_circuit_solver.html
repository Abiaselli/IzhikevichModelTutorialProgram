What the nullclines mean

The blue parabola is where the membrane voltage derivative is zero, so it separates regions where dv/dt is positive from regions where it is negative.

The green line is where the recovery derivative is zero, so it separates regions where du/dt rises from regions where it falls.

Their intersections are fixed points. Their geometry determines whether the neuron rests, resonates, spikes, or bursts.

Why spiking appears

The quadratic term 0.04v² bends the voltage nullcline into a parabola and creates a strongly accelerating right branch.

When the trajectory runs up that branch and crosses spike cutoff, the model stops following the smooth phase flow and applies the reset rule v ← c, u ← u + d.

That reset loop is what turns continuous dynamics into repetitive spikes and bursts.

Live interpretation

a

Recovery speed. Larger a makes the slow variable react faster.

b

Coupling slope. It sets the line u = bv and changes the fixed-point structure.

I

Applied current. Increasing I lifts the voltage nullcline and can move the system into sustained spiking.

c

Reset voltage after a spike. More negative c sends the state farther left after each reset.

d

Recovery jump after a spike. Larger d increases adaptation by making future spikes harder for a while.
